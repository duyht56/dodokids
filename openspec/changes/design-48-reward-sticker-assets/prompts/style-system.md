# Kido Reward Sticker Style System

## Shared prompt scaffold

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: bundled mobile game reward sticker
Primary request: create exactly one clearly recognizable sticker subject described by the item prompt
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid chroma-key background specified by the item; uniform edge-to-edge
Style/medium: polished soft-3D storybook illustration, rounded child-friendly proportions, matte-clay surfaces blended with clean digital paint, gentle highlights, subtle navy detail, thick warm-cream sticker rim
Composition/framing: one centered isolated subject, front or clear three-quarter view, complete silhouette, generous even padding, subject fills roughly 72-80 percent of the square
Lighting/mood: soft cheerful studio light contained entirely inside the subject; warm, safe and celebratory for children age 4-6
Constraints: preserve the item silhouette cue; no readable text, letters, numbers, logos or watermark; no scene, floor, cast shadow, contact shadow, reflection, loose particles or border crop; background is one perfectly uniform key color; do not use the key color anywhere in the subject or rim
Avoid: photorealism, flat clip art, thin fragile parts, clutter, complex scenery, excessive gloss, tiny decorative details, duplicate secondary characters, random props

## World accents

- World 1 — Khu vườn: mint/leaf green, sun yellow, coral and sky accents.
- World 2 — Đại dương: sky/teal/blue, coral, pearl cream and amber accents.
- World 3 — Vũ trụ: navy/lavender/diamond, sky blue, sun yellow and coral accents.
- World 4 — Hành trình: coral/amber/green, mountain blue, cream and lavender accents.

Natural subject color and recognizability override the accent palette. The four completion weeks are compact crest/emblems with two or three large motifs, never miniature scenes.

## Locked invariants

- All assets share the same warm-cream sticker rim thickness.
- One dominant silhouette per normal week.
- Completion crests use one circular or shield-like outer silhouette.
- No text is ever rendered inside the PNG.
- Eyes/faces appear only when natural for a friendly character or weather motif; ordinary objects are not all turned into mascots.
- Similar pairs must differ in shape, pose and identifying parts, not only hue.

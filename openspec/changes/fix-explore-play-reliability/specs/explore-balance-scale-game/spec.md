## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Lines cut by a pause are said again
A one-time gentle instruction (the exchange board's «Bé chạm vào quả … để đổi nhé», the release nudge, "now fill the pan") that a pause cancels SHALL be asked for again once play goes on, still as a gentle line (it waits for the question read again) and still only while it is wanted; a gentle line dropped because it is no longer wanted SHALL NOT be asked for again. The conclusion said when the board is solved SHALL be said again if a pause cut it off, and the board SHALL report only after it.

#### Scenario: Exit card during the exchange board's question
- **WHEN** the child opens the exit card while the question is read and comes back
- **THEN** the swap instruction is said after the question

#### Scenario: Pause during the conclusion
- **WHEN** the run is paused while the conclusion is said
- **THEN** after the pause the conclusion is said again, then the board moves on

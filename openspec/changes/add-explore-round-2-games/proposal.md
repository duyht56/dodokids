## Why

Đợt 1 (`fix-explore-catalog-round-1`) repaired the shared play layer of Khám phá.
The approved roadmap's next step is to make the catalog *fun to come back to*
and to cover three skill domains no game touches yet, without adding new art or
per-exercise content:

- The two highest-scoring new games from the review (12/15, 12/15):
  **Xếp tháp cho Đô Đô** (seriation by size/length/height/quantity — "xây rồi
  đổ" physical humour, Đô Đô climbs the child's tower) and **Ai lạc đàn?** (odd
  one out — the first game in the classification domain).
- Đô Đô's *voice* for feedback: today praise, retry, hints, level-up, run end
  and the break reminder are text a 4–6 year old cannot read. The catalog cannot
  say a game's name either.
- Support that actually helps: the play screen already escalates a hint level,
  but renderers ignore it. A child who misses four times still sees the same
  board; one who misses repeatedly is never offered an easier range.
- The product owner asked that **all new audio be generated in one batch**
  ("gom vào gen 1 thể"): one inventory, one `generate → review → approve →
  export` run producing pack `explore-audio-vi-v2`.

## What Changes

- **Two new local, offline, stateless games** registered end to end
  (contract codes, mobile registry/variety/renderer map, server registry,
  catalog groups): `stack_tower`, `odd_one_out`, each with a
  deterministic generator, independent validator, conformance script
  (`npm run test:explore-<game>`), kido-server spec and capability spec.
- **Đô Đô feedback voice** (best-effort, silent until the v2 pack lands): the
  play screen speaks rotating praise, gentle retry, the escalating hint, the
  range promotion line, "Mình thử bài dễ hơn nhé", the run-complete line and the
  break reminder; the catalog speaks a game's name when its card is tapped.
- **`supportLevel` renderer contract** (0/1/2 from the play screen's miss count)
  implemented in Khám phá số, Máy cộng trừ, Bên nào nhiều hơn and the three new
  games; **in-memory demotion** one range down after three misses in a row for
  range/arithmetic games.
- **One audio batch**: every new sentence (feedback ×13, 11 game names, 6
  number-bond feedback fragments, 6 tracing guidance lines, 5 + 1 new-game
  prompts, plus the 10 Route Planner feedback lines and `arith_remain_q` that
  were never bundled) is in the mobile inventory and the pipeline mirror; pack
  version bumps to `explore-audio-vi-v2` (mobile accepts v1 and v2).
- Conformance scripts for memory and pattern (`test:explore-memory`,
  `test:explore-pattern`).
- Catalog: four child-facing groups including the new games; a vector icon
  stands in for thumbnail art until the PNGs are delivered.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `explore-stack-tower-game`: seriation by size, length, height and quantity
  with physical lean/slide feedback and a completion climb.
- `explore-odd-one-out-game`: single-odd-token classification puzzles with
  noise levels and non-revealing hints.

### Modified Capabilities

- `explore-session-runtime`: spoken feedback voice, `supportLevel` contract,
  in-memory demotion, one-batch audio pack v2.
- `explore-catalog`: spoken game names, new groups, icon fallback.
- `explore-number-game`, `explore-arithmetic-game`,
  `explore-quantity-compare-game`: support-level visuals.
